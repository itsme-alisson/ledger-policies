Private personal use

Ledger is a personal budgeting and expense-tracking tool for its operator. It is not offered as a public banking or financial advisory service. Only the operator's own accounts may be connected under this installation's personal-use setup.

Bank authorization

Bank connections require authorization through the bank and Enable Banking. Their applicable terms and access restrictions also apply. Authorization can expire or be revoked, and reconnection may be necessary. Ledger does not provide payment initiation or hold funds.

Accuracy and availability

Imported data depends on the bank and provider. Records can be delayed, incomplete or subsequently updated. Categories, recurring-payment suggestions and forecasts are estimates that may need correction. Verify important amounts against official bank records. Ledger's reports are for personal organization and are not financial, investment or tax advice.

The application and banking connections may be unavailable during outages, maintenance or changes to provider access. There is no guaranteed synchronization time, availability or completeness of bank history.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for protecting the device, login credentials, banking key and backups; keeping the application updated; reviewing imported records; and complying with the bank and provider's conditions. The public policy repository must not contain financial records or application secrets.

Ending use and changes

The operator may stop using Ledger and revoke bank access at any time. Previously imported records and backups remain until separately deleted as described in the Privacy Policy. These notices should be updated when the installation's data handling or permitted use changes, with the revision date shown on this page.
