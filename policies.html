Privacy policy

Purpose and scope

Ledger is operated by Alisson (GitHub: itsme-alisson) for private, non-commercial use with the operator's own bank accounts. The application runs on the operator's computer. It does not offer public registration. This notice describes that installation and this public policy page.

Information processed

When the operator authorizes a bank connection, Ledger receives available account information, balances and transactions through Enable Banking. Depending on what the bank supplies, this includes account identifiers such as IBANs, currencies, transaction amounts and dates, descriptions, merchant or counterparty details, and transaction status.

Ledger also stores the owner's login email and password hash, login-session and security records, bank-authorization and synchronization records, and information entered for categories, budgets, planned expenses and savings goals.

How information is used

Information is used to import and categorize transactions, calculate balances and spending summaries, manage budgets and goals, and operate and secure the application. Banking access is read-only: Ledger does not initiate payments. Bank authentication takes place through the bank or provider's authorization flow; Ledger does not ask for or store the bank login password.

Ledger has no advertising or third-party usage analytics integration. Its financial calculations and categorization run locally, and the application does not send financial records to AI services or sell them.

Storage, security and retention

Imported records and planning information are stored in a PostgreSQL database controlled by the operator. Passwords are hashed and stored bank-session credentials are encrypted. Financial database records are not individually encrypted by the application; device and backup encryption are the operator's responsibility.

Imported financial history remains available until the operator deletes it. Disconnecting a bank stops further synchronization after a successful disconnection, but does not erase previously imported records. Removing retained data requires deleting the relevant local records or database and any backup copies. Ledger currently has no automatic financial-history deletion schedule.

Bank connections and external services

Enable Banking and the connected bank process information needed to authorize and provide account access under their own terms and privacy notices. Access depends on the accounts authorized and the consent's validity. The operator can disconnect through Ledger or manage and revoke authorization through the bank or provider where available.

See Enable Banking's privacy notice and the connected bank's privacy notice for their practices.

Cookies and this public page

The Ledger application uses an essential login-session cookie to maintain authenticated access. This policy page contains no analytics scripts, forms or application login, and does not access the local financial database.

When published on GitHub Pages, this page is hosted by GitHub. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Questions and data requests

Contact the operator using the details below for questions about Ledger's handling of information or requests concerning access, correction or deletion. Requests about information held independently by the bank, Enable Banking or GitHub should be directed to the relevant service.

Terms of use

Private personal use

Ledger is a personal budgeting and expense-tracking tool for its operator. It is not offered as a public banking or financial advisory service. Only the operator's own accounts may be connected under this installation's personal-use setup.

Bank authorization

Bank connections require authorization through the bank and Enable Banking. Their applicable terms and access restrictions also apply. Authorization can expire or be revoked, and reconnection may be necessary. Ledger does not provide payment initiation or hold funds.

Accuracy and availability

Imported data depends on the bank and provider. Records can be delayed, incomplete or subsequently updated. Categories, recurring-payment suggestions and forecasts are estimates that may need correction. Verify important amounts against official bank records. Ledger's reports are for personal organization and are not financial, investment or tax advice.

The application and banking connections may be unavailable during outages, maintenance or changes to provider access. There is no guaranteed synchronization time, availability or completeness of bank history.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for protecting the device, login credentials, banking key and backups; keeping the application updated; reviewing imported records; and complying with the bank and provider's conditions. The public policy repository must not contain financial records or application secrets.

Ending use and changes

The operator may stop using Ledger and revoke bank access at any time. Previously imported records and backups remain until separately deleted as described above. These notices should be updated when the installation's data handling or permitted use changes, with the revision date shown on this page.
